Frequently Asked Questions

A little guidance for your most treasured things.

Our Jewelry

What materials are used in your jewelry?

Our collections feature thoughtfully selected materials, including 925 sterling silver and 10k white and yellow gold, precious and semi-precious gemstones, and other materials specified in each product description.

We believe beautiful jewelry should be made to be worn, loved, and cherished. Each design is created with careful attention to detail, craftsmanship, and lasting beauty.

Is your jewelry made with genuine gemstones?

Our gemstone jewelry features natural or laboratory-created gemstones, depending on the design. This will be clearly notated in each listing. Each product description identifies the gemstones used, so you'll always know what makes your piece special.

Natural gemstones may have slight variations in color, clarity, and inclusions, making each one beautifully unique.

Is your jewelry hypoallergenic?

Many of our designs are crafted from 925 sterling silver, which is generally well tolerated by most wearers.

However, individual metal sensitivities vary, and we cannot guarantee that any piece will be completely hypoallergenic. If you have a known metal allergy, we recommend reviewing the materials listed in the product description before purchasing.

Will my sterling silver jewelry tarnish?

Sterling silver naturally reacts with elements in the environment and may develop tarnish over time. This is completely normal and does not indicate a defect.

With proper care and occasional polishing using a jewelry-safe cloth, your silver can maintain its beautiful shine for years to come.

How should I care for my jewelry?

We recommend removing your jewelry before swimming, showering, exercising, or sleeping. Avoid exposing your pieces to perfumes, lotions, cleaning products, and harsh chemicals.

When not being worn, store your jewelry separately in a cool, dry place. Gently polish sterling silver with a soft, jewelry-safe cloth, taking extra care around gemstones and delicate details.

Our Collections

What is the difference between Botanicals and The Atelier?

Botanicals is inspired by the beauty of the natural world, particularly in bridal bouquets. From delicate blossoms to graceful branches, these sculptural designs capture the romance of a garden in bloom.

The Atelier celebrates the beauty of gemstones through one-of-a-kind and small-batch jewelry. Each design is thoughtfully created to highlight the character, color, and individuality of its stones.

Both collections share our love of old-world elegance, meaningful details, and timeless beauty.

Are your designs limited edition?

Some of our designs are produced in small batches, while others may be one-of-a-kind.

Because certain pieces feature individually selected gemstones or intricate detailing, availability may be limited. When applicable, this will be noted in the product description.

Will sold-out designs be restocked?

Some of our most-loved designs may return, while others are created in limited quantities and may not be reproduced.

If you've fallen in love with a piece that's currently unavailable, we encourage you to check its product page for restock information or reach out through our Contact Form.

Where is your jewelry made?

Our jewelry is designed in New York and produced in collaboration with professional jewelry manufacturers and gem dealers located in the United States.

We carefully consider materials, craftsmanship, and finishing to ensure that each piece reflects the character and quality of our designs.

Limited Warranty

Limited Warranty

Cédille & Co. LLC provides a one (1) year limited warranty from the date of delivery on all completed pieces manufactured through its third-party partners.

This warranty covers:

  • Material defects or workmanship issues.
  • Significant discrepancies between the final piece and the client-approved design.
  • One (1) standard resizing, where applicable.

If a covered issue arises, Cédille & Co. LLC will coordinate repair or correction with our third-party manufacturer, including arranging expedited shipping where reasonably possible.

This warranty does not cover:

  • Normal wear and tear.
  • Accidental damage, misuse, or improper care.
  • Third-party alterations or repairs.
  • Loss or theft.
  • Damage to client-supplied stones or materials.

All warranty claims must be submitted within the one (1) year period and may require inspection prior to approval.

Cédille & Co. LLC’s responsibility under this warranty is limited to facilitating repair or correction through its manufacturing partners and does not guarantee specific timelines or outcomes.

Appraisals

Appraisals

Cédille & Co. LLC offers the option to include a detailed, in-house appraisal with your piece for an additional $250, invoiced separately. This document provides an expert evaluation of authenticity and market value, establishing an appropriate replacement value for insurance purposes.

While many clients choose this service for convenience, you are equally welcome and encouraged to arrange an appraisal independently if preferred. For those who elect to include it, each appraisal is conducted by qualified professionals and reflects the craftsmanship, materials, and fine details unique to your design—serving as both a record of your piece and a foundation for securing proper coverage.

Jewelry Insurance

Jewelry Insurance

We recommend insuring your jewelry to protect both its financial and sentimental value. Using your appraisal, coverage can be easily arranged through specialized insurers such as Jewelers Mutual, with policies that typically include protection against loss, damage, and theft.

Insurance is a considered step in preserving your piece, allowing it to be worn and enjoyed with confidence. A well-structured policy ensures that your jewelry remains protected for years to come.
