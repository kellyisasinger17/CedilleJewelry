What guides us

Our Values

✦

Find beauty in the everyday.

We believe jewelry should evoke emotion, tell stories, and celebrate life.

Create with intention and longevity.

We value quality over quantity, through the study of the art of adornment.

Champion American craftsmanship.

We are committed to supporting American artisans and responsible, domestic production.

Honor tradition.

Our French heritage drives our appreciation for intentionality and quality. We honor the past by reimagining its beauty for the present.

Our Story

A little nostalgia, a little romance.

You know the feeling.

You've always found a little magic in the things others might overlook. You believe there's a story behind everything beautiful, and you've probably fallen hopelessly in love with a fictional character or two along the way. (No judgment here.)

I've always been drawn to the beauty of other worlds, whether in nature, mythology, or classical literature. Cédille & Co. was born from that fascination: a love of beautiful things, meaningful details, and the stories that make them unforgettable.

Each of our designs is a little celebration of romance and imagination, thoughtfully created to bring a touch of that magic into everyday life.
After all, who says a little old-world romance can't belong in the modern world?

There's still plenty of beauty to be found.

With love,
Shay

Jewelry for the incurably romantic.
